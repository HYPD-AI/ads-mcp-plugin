# Getting started with HYPD

1. **Create a HYPD account** at https://app.hypd.ai (OAuth sign-in, no card needed to start).
2. **Connect at least one source** on the Sources page (https://app.hypd.ai/sources): Google Ads, Meta, Google Analytics, or Merchant Center.
3. **Authorize the plugin** — in the terminal: run `/mcp`, select **hypd**, choose **Authenticate**, approve in the browser. In the desktop app or Cowork: Settings → **Customize** → **Plugins** → open **HYPD AI - Paid Ads & Analytics** → connect the **HYPD AI Ads** connector. An existing HYPD authorization on claude.ai is reused automatically.
4. **Verify** with: "List my Google Ads accounts."

Full walkthrough with screenshots: https://docs.hypd.ai/guides/claude-code-plugin
